Install OpenQ

In a terminal:

curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -fsSL https://github.com/iagonunez/openqup/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Installs the build tools from your distribution's packages (it asks first), builds OpenQ and adds it to your applications menu.

In a terminal:

curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -fsSL https://github.com/iagonunez/openqup/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

macOS 11 or later. On a Mac without Apple's Command Line Tools, macOS asks to install them first; run the command again afterwards. OpenQ ends up in Applications in your home folder.

In PowerShell:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://github.com/iagonunez/openqup/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex"

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Without Microsoft's C++ Build Tools, their installer opens first: accept it and let it finish. OpenQ is then in the Start menu.

The installer, openqup, builds OpenQ on your computer. Later: openqup update · openqup uninstall.

What it does

15 cue typesAudio, video, text, camera, live mic, groups, waits, fades, OSC and more.
Sample-accurate timingPre- and post-waits, auto-continue and auto-follow, chained to the exact sample.
Mixer, EQ and effectsRouting matrix, 8-band EQ, reverb, delay, dynamics and more, edited live.
Object audioPlace sounds in a room of speakers and fly them around with fades.
Video and projectionMovies, stills, SVG and titles on any number of stages, with mapping and edge blending.
Animation and video effectsKeyframes, blend modes, colour effects and chroma key, drawn live by the GPU.
Timecode and OSCFollow LTC or MTC, take OSC from other gear and send it to lighting desks.
Remote devicesRun or edit the show from a laptop or tablet over an encrypted link.
Built for the boothShow mode, autosave and crash recovery, audio that reconnects by itself.

Everything is explained step by step in the user manual, which also comes with the app (Help → User manual, or F1).
